## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

## 5. Layout Principles

**Spacing scale:** 1px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 25px, 26px, 40px, 64px

**Base unit:** 8px grid (values are mostly multiples of 8).

**Border radii:** 2px, 4px, 7px, 8px, 16px, 18px, 50%, 100px

## 6. Guidelines

### Do
- Use 8px grid for all spacing
- Use `#6363F1` for primary actions and CTAs
- Stick to 2 font weights: 400, 500
- Use `Untitled Sans` as the primary typeface

### Don't
- Don't introduce colors outside the palette above
- Don't mix fonts beyond Untitled Sans and IBM Plex Mono
- Don't use inline styles when the design system covers the pattern
- Don't use border-radius values outside: 2px, 4px, 7px, 8px, 16px, 18px, 50%, 100px
